Frequently Asked Questions

Transparency First

Everything you need to know about Nuvia's methodology, privacy, and scientific standards.

Is Nuvia really completely free? ▼

Yes! Nuvia's core scanning engine, allergen warnings, E-code database, and alternative suggestions are 100% free with no hidden charges.

How are NuviaScores™ calculated? Can brands sponsor scores? ▼

Brands cannot pay or sponsor their scores under any circumstances. Scores are computed automatically based on toxicological monographs published by the European Food Safety Authority (EFSA), World Health Organization (WHO), and US FDA.

Does the app work in supermarket basements with no internet? ▼

Yes. Nuvia includes an offline safety engine containing 10,000+ local products and 450+ chemical additives stored directly on your phone.

Can Nuvia protect against severe allergies? ▼

You can set your allergen profile for gluten, lactose, peanuts, soy, sulfites, and tree nuts. The app flags matching ingredients immediately with high-priority banners.

Which devices and operating systems are supported? ▼

Nuvia is available for all modern Android phones running Android 7.0 and above, directly on the Google Play Store.

🔒

Privacy by Design

We believe your health and dietary choices belong only to you. Nuvia does not track your location, does not harvest your personal shopping lists, and never sells user profiles to advertising brokers. All image scanning is processed locally on your device.
